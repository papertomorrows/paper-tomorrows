## Description
A hollow sphere 150 metres across. By day, sunlight through tiny holes turned the inside into a sky full of stars. By night, a giant glowing orb hung in the centre.

French architect Étienne-Louis Boullée designed this cenotaph for Isaac Newton in 1784. It was never built. Almost 240 years later, a sphere of nearly the same size opened in Las Vegas. This is a concept visualization of what might have been.
