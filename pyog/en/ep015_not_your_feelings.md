So how can you feel this deeply without drowning in it? Think of an actor.

A good actor really cries in a sad scene. They feel that sadness with their whole body. But when the director calls cut, they wipe their tears and come back to themselves. The actor experienced the role. They didn't become it.

We're on the stage of this Earth too, playing countless emotional scenes. Feel them deeply, but remember that the role isn't you.

Here's how it works in daily life. When we're angry, we usually say, I'm angry. In that moment, it feels like you and the anger are one.
