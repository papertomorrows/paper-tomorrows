Two kilometres high. A million residents. Skyscrapers hanging inside the frame.

Japan's Shimizu Corporation designed the Mega-City Pyramid for Tokyo Bay: a 2,004-metre structure with suspended 30-storey towers and transit pods running through its struts. It needs materials that are still being researched, and it was never built. This is a concept visualization of what might have been.
