## Description
Four hundred metres of iron and glass. Three buildings inside, each turning at its own speed: once a year, once a month, once a day.

Vladimir Tatlin designed the Monument to the Third International in 1919-1920 for Petrograd (now Saint Petersburg). It was never built. This is a concept visualization of what might have been.
