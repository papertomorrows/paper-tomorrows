# EP016 (EN) There Is No "Just Because"

When you open the fridge, take nothing out, and close it again. When you unlock your phone, forget what you were going to do, and just keep scrolling. An evening when your heart sinks for no reason at all. We cover every one of these moments with the same words. Just because.

But the truth is, there is no just because. Today, I'll talk about how simply noticing these two small words can begin to change your life.

And I'll tell you the story of someone whose heart always grew heavy around one particular smell, and how they found the real reason behind their just because.

Our own just becauses are the same.

On the way home from work, you're irritated for no reason. Look closely, and you skipped a real lunch, and in a meeting, what you said was brushed aside like it was nothing. The truth is, you were exhausted, and you wanted someone to understand how you felt.

You just met someone, and you don't like them, for no reason. Look closely, and maybe the way they talk reminds you of someone who once hurt you.

Things are going well, and still, you feel anxious for no reason. Look closely, and maybe there's a very young you inside, who believed you had to do well to be loved.

<!-- section: inward | visuals: woman looking inward closed eyes, crowd city rush hour, phone screen scrolling night, woman meditating light, door light dark room | title: Turn Your Gaze Inward | ai: a person standing in a vast grid-like city of glowing green code and endless screens, turning away from it with eyes closed, a soft golden doorway opening at the center of their own chest >> the glowing code city flickers and slowly dissolves around them, the golden doorway in their chest opens wider and pours out warm light -->
But we rarely look at that story.

We're usually far more sensitive to what's outside. Other people's faces, other people's words, the news of the world. All day long, our attention points outward.

In a single day, you check other people's updates dozens of times. But how many times did you ask your own heart, how was today?

We're numb to what's actually happening inside us. And that's where just because comes from.

Now it's time to change the order. Inside first, before outside. And more of it. Becoming more sensitive, more particular, about knowing yourself. Where did this feeling come from? Why did I say that? Not letting it slip by.

People on the spiritual path sometimes call this escaping the Matrix. The thoughts the world decided for you, the beliefs others planted in you, the reactions that repeat without you noticing. Living inside all that, without knowing why, just because. That is the Matrix.

But the door out of the Matrix isn't outside. The only way out is in. The more you come to know yourself, the further you walk out of the Matrix.

<!-- section: aware | visuals: woman journaling night, lantern dark forest, woman eyes closed calm, flashlight dark cave, sunrise window | title: Ask Why, Gently | ai: a person holding a small glowing lantern, walking calmly down a stone staircase into a dark inner cave, the lantern light revealing old glowing words and memories carved into the walls >> the lantern light slowly reveals more glowing memories on the cave walls as the person descends calmly, warm light spreads -->
So how do you go in? Do what the person with the smell of disinfectant did.

When the words just because come up, pause once and ask. Why?

Not like you're scolding yourself, but gently, like asking a curious friend. And when an answer comes, ask once more. And why is that?

For example. I just put it off. Why? I didn't want to do it. Why? Honestly, it felt overwhelming. And why is that? I was afraid I'd fail.

Ask just three times, and the real feeling hiding behind just because shows itself.

If the answer doesn't come right away, don't rush. Wait. Sometimes your body answers before your thoughts do. A tightness in your chest, a lump in your throat, your shoulders stiffening.

That is self-awareness.

Awareness isn't only for when you meditate. When you eat, when you speak, when you write someone back, when you walk. In every moment of the day, being just a little more awake.

Oh, I'm thinking this right now. Oh, I'm about to say this. Oh, I'm sighing again.

You don't have to be perfect. Just notice one more time today than you did yesterday. As those awake moments grow, just because fades, and the inside of you grows brighter. Awakening isn't somewhere far away. It's coming to know, one piece at a time, the parts of you that you didn't know.
