## Description
A dam across the Strait of Gibraltar. A sea up to 200 metres lower. More than 600,000 square kilometres of new land.

In the late 1920s, German architect Herman Sörgel began promoting Atlantropa: a plan to dam the Strait of Gibraltar and partly drain the Mediterranean Sea (about 100 metres in the west, 200 in the east), with hydroelectric power for Europe and Africa. It was never built. This is a concept visualization of what might have been.
