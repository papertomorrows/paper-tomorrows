# Short #008 - Time Travel #2: The Titanic

## Title
I Had One Night to Save the Titanic | Time Travel #2

## Description
I time traveled onto the Titanic on April 14, 1912. I knew how it would end.

What's real: seven ice warnings reached Titanic's wireless operators. The lookouts had no binoculars. The ship was doing about 22 knots when lookout Frederick Fleet rang the bell three times and reported "Iceberg, right ahead!" at 11:40 p.m. Ice fell onto the deck. The first lifeboat left with about 28 people aboard; it could hold 65. There were 20 lifeboats for more than 2,200 people. The band kept playing. The nearest ship, the Californian, was roughly 10-12 miles away, and its radio operator had switched off his set and gone to bed. The Titanic sank at 2:20 a.m. on April 15. About 1,500 people died.

What's dramatized: my visit, my warnings, the officer's words and the countdown. Visuals are AI-generated, not real footage.
