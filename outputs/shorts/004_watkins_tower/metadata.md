## Description
Taller than the Eiffel Tower. A hotel, theatres and Turkish baths in the sky.

In the 1890s, railway entrepreneur Sir Edward Watkin began building a tower at Wembley Park, planned to rise about 46 metres higher than the Eiffel Tower. The money ran out and the foundations subsided at 47 metres. The stump was demolished with dynamite in 1904-1907, and Wembley Stadium now stands on the site. This is a concept visualization of what might have been.
