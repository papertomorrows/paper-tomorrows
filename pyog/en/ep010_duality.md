# EP010 (EN) The Moment You Realize There's No Good or Bad | Where There's Only Light, Light Can't Be Known

A life with only good things. Only joy, and no sorrow at all.

If that life existed, would you really be happy?

Surprisingly, no. In a world like that, we couldn't even know what happiness is.

Today, I'll tell you why. Why light and darkness, good and bad, life and death have to exist together. And how, the moment you understand it, an almost absurd freedom begins, where no feeling scares you anymore.

<!-- section: duality | visuals: candle flame dark, day night timelapse, ice and fire, sunrise over darkness, stars night sky | title: Where There's Only Light, Light Can't Be Known | ai: a cold glowing blue crystal and a warm golden flame floating side by side in deep darkness, the blue making the gold look warmer and the gold making the blue look colder, both shining beautifully >> the blue crystal and the golden flame slowly orbit each other, their light mixing and pulsing, sparks drift in the darkness -->
Imagine a world with only cold. In that world, could we know what cold is?

We couldn't. With no warmth to compare, the word cold, the feeling of cold, wouldn't exist.

There's a story like this. Long ago, in a place made entirely of light, there was a little light. The little light knew it was light. But it had never once felt it. Because everything around it was light too. Light inside light can't be seen.

So the little light made a wish. I want to truly experience that I am light. And for that, it needed something that was not light. It needed darkness.

Many spiritual teachings say this is exactly why our souls came to Earth. Earth is a world split in two, a world of duality. Light and darkness. Being and not-being. Like and dislike. Right and wrong. Good and evil. Cold and hot. Meeting and parting. And life and death.

Because there is light, there is darkness. Because there is right, there is wrong. I can say I like something only because there are things I dislike. If only one of each pair existed, it couldn't have the value and meaning we know. With only good, we wouldn't know good. With only life, we wouldn't know what it means to be alive.

Knowing something and experiencing it are different. So we meet parting to experience love. We get bound to experience freedom. We come to a place with death to experience life.

Without what you are not, you can't experience who you are.

So don't hate the darkness. Darkness isn't a supporting role for light. It's another experience, just as precious as light.

At the source this world came from, everything simply is. There's no good and no bad. The labels good and bad are what we, experiencing this world of duality, put on things.

There's an old Chinese story. An old farmer's horse ran away, and the villagers said, what bad luck. The horse came back with a wild horse, and they said, what good luck. His son fell from it and broke his leg. Bad luck. Then soldiers came to take young men to war, and his son was spared.

So which was good? There's no right answer.

Good or bad, valuable or not, isn't fixed. We, living this life on Earth, are choosing to feel it that way. So whatever has happened, you can choose what it means. That's the greatest power we have in this game.

<!-- section: dream | visuals: misty forest dream, reflection lake, light rays clouds, milky way, soap bubble light | title: Real, and Also a Dream | ai: a person standing on a mirror-still lake at dawn, the sky and its reflection merging so perfectly that the horizon disappears, soft floating bubbles of light drifting around them >> the bubbles of light slowly drift upward and some gently burst into sparkles, mist moves across the mirror lake, dawn light grows -->
The joy, the tears, the love you feel right now are all real. Feel them for real. Live them for real.

But at the same time, all of this is a dream. The Diamond Sutra says: all conditioned things are like a dream, an illusion, a bubble, a shadow.

From the eyes of your eternal self, this lifetime is a vivid dream we've stepped into for a while. A game.

Picture a player completely absorbed in a game. They laugh, cry, take on every challenge with their whole heart. But they know: this is a game. So when they lose, they don't collapse. When they fall, they get back up.

Someone who knows it's real and also a dream lives the most freely, the most as themselves.

<!-- section: accept | visuals: woman sitting quietly window, rain on window, calm lake, person walking forest path, sunrise field | title: Whatever You Feel, That's You Too | ai: a person sitting peacefully in the middle of swirling ribbons of many colors of light, some bright gold, some deep blue and violet, letting all of them flow around them without pushing any away >> the colored ribbons of light slowly swirl and flow around the calm seated figure, none pushed away, soft glow pulses gently -->
But there's something I really want to say here.

Once you understand this, you no longer have to force yourself to think positively. You're still alive, so be grateful. Always stay positive. That mindset can be just another game someone else made.

What matters most is noticing the feelings and thoughts that come up. And asking: is this truly mine? Or is it a thought I inherited from someone else?

And whatever it is, accepting it. Sadness, fear, anger, it's all okay. You don't have to turn it into something nicer. That's you too.

We are everything that exists. Positive and negative, light and dark, it's all us.

There are nights when you're alone and your heart sinks for no reason. Usually we try to get rid of that feeling fast. We pick up the phone, we think about something else.

Instead, look at it like this. Ah, I'm experiencing this feeling right now. I'm experiencing this thought right now. Whatever it is, you watch it, you feel it, and you let it move through you.

Someone who accepts every feeling as part of themselves becomes almost absurdly free, even while living in a body on this Earth. Because they're no longer at war with their own feelings. Even here on Earth, experiencing life in a body, they remember who they are, who we are. And little by little, they become more and more themselves.

When you exist as yourself like that, you start walking the path that was prepared for you. Existing with a happy soul, doing what your soul came into this life to do, and creating the value only you can bring. That's the life you get to experience, here on Earth.

And this isn't just about one person. When each of us exists as ourselves like that, this world will become a completely different place. I dream of the day all of humanity lives that way.

<!-- section: practice | visuals: morning journal, sunrise window, woman sitting quietly window, walking nature, calm night bedroom | title: Two Things to Try Starting Today -->
So starting today, try just two things.

First, when a feeling or thought comes up, first ask: is this truly mine? And whatever it is, don't push it away. Accept it.

Second, before you sleep, bring to mind one feeling that came up today, and tell it: yes, that's me too.

<!-- section: together | visuals: candle light, calm lake, stars night sky, light rays clouds, sunrise ocean | title: Let's Try It Together -->
So let's take one minute together, right now.

Sit comfortably and close your eyes. Breathe in slowly… and breathe out, long.

What feeling is coming up right now? Sadness, fear, stillness, just let it be. You don't have to change it.

Simply watch it. Ah, I'm experiencing this feeling right now.

And ask: is this feeling truly mine?

Whatever it is, accept it. That's who you are right now, too.

Now, take a deep breath in… and as you slowly breathe out, open your eyes.

Good and bad aren't fixed, and everything is real, and also a dream. So accept every feeling as yours, and live more as yourself. On the path prepared for you, doing what your soul wants to do.
